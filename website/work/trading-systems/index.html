Autonomous Trading Robots

Discipline / Autonomy

Rules decided in the calm, executed in the noise.

The hardest part of any system isn't the decision — it's holding to it when the moment arrives.

These are systems that act on a strategy already reasoned through, and then remove the one unreliable part: hesitation. The judgement happens up front, in the quiet. What runs afterward is discipline made mechanical.

The interesting work is everything around the trade — measuring, guarding, and knowing exactly when not to act. A system you can trust is mostly a system that knows its own limits.
