The Seerah for Children

Story / Faith

The oldest story, told so the youngest can carry it.

A life that changed the world, brought within reach of a child's attention.

The life of the Prophet ﷺ is retold with the care it asks for and the pace a young audience needs — faithful to the sources, gentle in the telling, and built to be watched and returned to.

The craft is in restraint: what to keep, what to hold back, and how to let a story that is centuries old feel like it is being told for the first time.
