CastAdhan

Time / Devotion

A home that remembers, from the first call before dawn to the last at night.

There was a time when the call to prayer set the rhythm of the day, without anyone having to look at a clock.

CastAdhan brings that rhythm back — quietly. A small device works alongside the speakers and screens a household already owns, and fills the home with the adhan at the right moment for its place on earth, all year round.

Every room can be its own volume. Every sound — the call, recitation, the remembrance at day's end — sits where it belongs. And when the moment isn't right, a single tap stops it, from any phone in the house.

No apps. No accounts. No subscriptions. Once set up, it simply takes care of the rest — and improves itself overnight while the house sleeps.
