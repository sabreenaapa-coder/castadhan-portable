Mosque Timetable Creator

Order / Community

A year of prayer times, composed — not copied out by hand.

Every mosque needs one, and almost every one is made the hard way.

This tool computes the whole year — begins and jamā‘ah times, the rules a community chooses to keep — and lays them out into something ready for the wall, the pocket and the screen. Change one setting; the entire year re-composes itself.

The work is in the details a printed timetable hides: the calculation method, the rounding conventions, the small local decisions that make a timetable feel like this mosque's, and not a generic one.
