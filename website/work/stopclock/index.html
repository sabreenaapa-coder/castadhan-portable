IV

STOPCLOCK

Play / Synchrony

One clock, one room, everyone moving against the same second.

A party game where the clock is the opponent — and it never blinks.

Words and numbers arrive; the pressure is the ticking. Everyone in the room shares one live timer, so a turn is never really private — the whole table feels the same countdown.

The engineering is invisible on purpose: every screen has to agree on the same instant, or the game falls apart. Get that right, and a phone in each hand becomes a single, shared board.
