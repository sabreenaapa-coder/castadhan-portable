GOBBLE

Pattern / Perception

Between any two players, exactly one symbol matches. See it first.

A game built on a small, beautiful piece of mathematics — and the split-second where the eye beats the mind to it.

Every card carries a handful of symbols. Any two cards you can lay side by side share one — and only one — in common. The design is quiet; the play is fast. The whole table is racing to see the same thing.

Underneath the fun is a structure with no room for error: a geometry where coincidence is guaranteed. Above it, a game that feels like reflex.
