The 24-Hour Prayer Clock

In Your Hands

Simple Everyday Control

Whenever you wish, personalise your home’s schedule, adjust audio or pause playback using the CastAdhan control page from any phone or tablet.

Large, clear controls make everyday changes simple without getting in the way. Add the dashboard straight to your phone’s home screen like an app for instant access.

A single, one-tap Stop button lets you pause quietly if the moment isn’t right — full control, without fumbling through menus.

10
Getting Started

Ready in Three Minutes

  1. Unbox your CastAdhan. Connect the included Ethernet cable to your home router, then plug in the power. Wait one minute.
  2. On your phone or tablet, open castadhan.local:8786 in your browser.
  3. A simple welcome wizard guides you through connecting your Wi-Fi and choosing your city. Once done, you can even unplug the Ethernet cable — CastAdhan carries on over Wi-Fi.

No apps to download. No accounts to create.
No terminal commands.
