Setup Guide
Three minutes, three steps.
Assalamu alaikum

This little box will fill your home with the adhan, played through your Google speakers. Setting it up takes about three minutes — and you only ever do it once.

In the box

  • Your CastAdhan boxa small Raspberry Pi computer, already assembled in its case
  • Power cable
  • Ethernet cableonly needed for the first few minutes

You’ll need

  • Your home Wi-Fi
  • One Google Cast or Nest speakersuch as Nest Mini, Nest Audio, Nest Hub or Google TV — Amazon Alexa devices are not supported
2
Step One
1

Connect

Plug the Ethernet cable into the CastAdhan box, and the other end into a spare socket on your home router — the box your internet comes from.

Plug in the power cable.

Wait one minute. There’s no screen and nothing to press — the box quietly starts up by itself.

3
Step Two
2

Open

Make sure your phone is connected to your home Wi-Fi.

Then open the web browser on your phone or tablet, and in the address bar type:

http://castadhan.local:8786

A welcome page appears, greeting you with Assalamu alaikum.

4
Step Three
3

The Welcome Wizard

The wizard walks you through, one small step at a time:

  1. Where you are — choose your city.
  2. How times are calculated — if unsure, leave the defaults.
  3. Your speakers — tick the rooms that should play the adhan, and hear a test adhan if you’d like.

Rather not leave the cable in? Open Settings → 📶 Wi-Fi Setup, join your home Wi-Fi, then unplug the Ethernet — CastAdhan carries on wirelessly.

No apps to download. No accounts to create.
No terminal commands.

5
If Something Doesn’t Work

Don’t Worry

“My phone can’t find castadhan.local.”
Check your phone is on your home Wi-Fi, and give the box its full minute. If it still doesn’t appear, ask your gift-giver to text you the box’s address, and type that in instead.
“I can’t hear anything from the box itself.”
That’s normal — the box itself is silent. The adhan plays through your Google speakers, just like casting music.
“The wizard can’t find my speakers.”
Check they’re powered on and on the same Wi-Fi, then tap Scan again. Remember it works with Google Cast and Nest speakers — Amazon Alexa doesn’t work.
“My Wi-Fi has changed.”
Tell your gift-giver — they can update the box remotely. You don’t have to do anything.
6
Every Day

Living with CastAdhan

The dashboard is your remote control. Open it from any phone in the house, and add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

One tap to pause. If the moment isn’t right — a phone call, a sleeping baby — the Stop button quietly pauses playback.

The clock on your TV. The 📺 TV Display tab shows you how to put the 24-hour prayer clock on your television.

It looks after itself. The box updates its own software overnight while your family sleeps. You never need to plug it into a computer.
