## A Home That Remembers Allah

There was once a time when the call to prayer naturally marked the rhythm of everyday life.

The adhan echoed through towns and villages, reminding hearts to turn towards Allah. Children grew up hearing it. Families paused together. The remembrance of Allah became part of ordinary life.

CastAdhan is designed to gently bring that feeling back into your home.

# Every Prayer

### The Adhan, Beautifully Delivered

CastAdhan automatically plays the adhan for each of the five daily prayers, precisely timed for your location throughout the year.

Whether you choose one room or your entire home, every speaker can have its own independent volume.

And because every type of sound serves a different purpose, CastAdhan also lets you adjust the Adhan, Qur’an recitation, and daily Dhikr independently—so the Fajr call can gently wake the house while the bedtime remembrance plays softly in the children’s room. Every sound, perfectly balanced for your family.

**And it can even be your own voice** — record the adhan yourself, so your family is called to prayer by someone who loves them.

# Beautiful Every Day

### The 24‑Hour Prayer Clock

Display an elegant prayer clock on your television or smart display and see the entire day at a glance.

Featuring a striking **single‑hand 24‑hour dial**, each prayer carries its own custom scene and light—the current hour softly glows over a shifting aurora, transforming time from numbers into a visual landscape. The live countdown to the next prayer, Hijri date and local weather help everyone in the home—from young children to grandparents—understand the day’s journey with a single glance.

---

### Simple Everyday Control

Whenever you wish, personalise your home’s schedule, adjust audio or pause playback using the CastAdhan control page from any phone or tablet.

Large, clear controls make everyday changes simple without getting in the way. **Add the dashboard straight to your phone’s home screen like an app** for instant access. A single, one‑tap **Stop** button lets you pause quietly if the moment isn’t right—giving you full control without fumbling through menus.

---

> ## Getting Started in Three Minutes
>
> Unbox your CastAdhan. **Connect the included Ethernet cable to your home router**, then plug in the power. Wait one minute.
>
> On your phone or tablet, open **http://castadhan.local:8786** in your browser.
>
> A simple welcome wizard guides you through connecting your Wi‑Fi and choosing your city. Once done, you can even **unplug the Ethernet cable** — CastAdhan carries on over Wi‑Fi.
>
> **No apps to download. No accounts to create. No terminal commands.**
>
> ---
>
> ## What You’ll Need
>
> To enjoy CastAdhan in your home, you will need:
>
> ✓ CastAdhan Box *(included — the quiet brain that does all the work)*
>
> ✓ Home Wi‑Fi
>
> ✓ At least one compatible Google Cast speaker or display
> *(such as Nest Mini, Nest Audio, Nest Hub or Google TV)*
>
> Amazon Alexa devices are not currently supported.

CastAdhan automatically updates its software overnight, every day at 4 a.m.

Built on open‑source software with no ongoing subscription costs, it is designed to serve Muslim homes for many years to come.

If you ever need help, someone you trust can securely assist with your settings from anywhere, bringing lasting peace of mind.
