## Back side (small print)

**What's in the box**
- Raspberry Pi computer (about the size of a credit card)
- Plastic case (already assembled)
- USB-C / micro-USB power cable

**What you'll need**
- A free wall socket
- Your home WiFi (we've already loaded your network details)
- At least one Google Cast / Nest / Chromecast speaker on the same WiFi

**Frequently asked**

*"What if my phone can't find `castadhan.local`?"*  
Try `http://castadhan.local:8786` instead. If that still doesn't work, ask your gift-giver to text you the box's IP address.

*"What if my WiFi changes?"*  
Tell your gift-giver — they'll update the box remotely or post you a new SD card.

*"How does it know prayer times?"*  
It checks your location automatically and downloads accurate times every night.

*"How does it play the adhan?"*  
Through your Google smart speakers — same as casting music. The Pi itself is silent.

*"Will it update itself?"*  
Yes — automatically, daily at 4 a.m. You don't have to do anything.

## Printing notes

- Recommended size: **A6** (105 × 148 mm) for tabletop boxes, **A5** for larger
- Recommended paper: thick matte card (200–300 gsm)
- Recommended print colour: black text on cream / kraft paper
- Round the corners for a polished look
- If sending internationally, include the URL in English even if the rest is translated
